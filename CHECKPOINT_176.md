# Checkpoint 176 — Powers of a Diagonalizable Matrix

This lesson delivers the computational payoff of diagonalization.

## Mathematical arc

1. Recall `A = P D P^{-1}`.
2. Expand `A^2` and show the cancellation `P^{-1}P = I`.
3. Generalize to `A^k = P D^k P^{-1}`.
4. Compute `D^4` for the continuing 3x3 example.
5. Reconstruct `A^4` and verify the numerical result.
6. Contrast diagonal powering with repeated full matrix multiplication.
7. Interpret repeated powers through the eigenvalue factors `1^k`, `2^k`, and `5^k`.

The final card bridges naturally toward repeated transformations and dominant-eigenvalue behavior without yet starting the dynamics lesson.
