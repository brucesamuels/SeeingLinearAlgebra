# Checkpoint 183 — Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors Chapter Review

This checkpoint adds the final review lesson before Chapter 7 assembly.

The review revisits the chapter in six synthesis cards:

1. the conceptual arc from special directions to applications;
2. eigenpairs, the characteristic equation, and eigenspaces as null spaces;
3. eigenvector bases, diagonalization, and powers;
4. repeated eigenvalues and the diagonalizability criterion;
5. symmetric matrices, orthogonal eigenvectors, and the spectral theorem;
6. repeated dynamics, first-order ODE systems, and Fibonacci-type difference equations.

No new mathematics is introduced. The purpose is consolidation before full chapter assembly.
