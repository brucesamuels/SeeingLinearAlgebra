# Checkpoint 57 — Three-Vector Addition in 3-Space

## Visual refinement

This refinement strengthens the 3D readability of the accepted CP57 lesson.

Changes:

- replace the original vectors with a more visually spread triple,
  \[
  \mathbf u=(3,0,1),\qquad
  \mathbf v=(0,3,1),\qquad
  \mathbf w=(1,1,3),
  \]
  so that
  \[
  \mathbf u+\mathbf v+\mathbf w=(4,4,5).
  \]
- widen the camera angle to better reveal spatial separation,
- rotate the camera longer and faster after the parallelepiped appears,
- keep the parallelepiped, resultant vector, and exact sum visible in the
  closing reflection frame,
- make the conclusion explicit: the sum is the **body diagonal** of the
  parallelepiped.

No mathematical architecture changes are introduced. The refinement affects
only presentation details and their regression tests.
