# Checkpoint 76 — Basis: Enough, but Not Too Many

## Goal
Show that a basis is a spanning set with no redundant vectors, and that dimension counts the number of basis directions.

## Lesson structure
- Start with three vectors in 3-space that generate a plane.
- Make the redundancy visible by showing that \(\mathbf v_3=\mathbf v_1+\mathbf v_2\).
- Reveal the plane generated by the vectors.
- Ask whether all three vectors are really needed.
- Fade out the redundant vector while the plane remains unchanged.
- Conclude that
  \[
  \operatorname{span}\{\mathbf v_1,\mathbf v_2,\mathbf v_3\}
  =
  \operatorname{span}\{\mathbf v_1,\mathbf v_2\}.
  \]
- Identify \(\{\mathbf v_1,\mathbf v_2\}\) as a basis.
- Conclude that the dimension of the plane is 2.
