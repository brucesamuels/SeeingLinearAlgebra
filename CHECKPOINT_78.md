# Checkpoint 78 — Pivot Columns: A Basis from the Original Matrix

## Goal
Show that pivot positions are read from the row-echelon form, but the basis vectors for the column space must be taken from the original matrix.

## Lesson structure
- Display the original matrix \(A\) and its row-echelon form \(R\).
- Draw the three columns of \(A\) as vectors in \(\mathbb R^3\).
- Reveal the column-space plane generated by the first two columns.
- Show the dependency relation
  \[
  \mathbf a_3=-\mathbf a_1+\mathbf a_2.
  \]
- Highlight the pivot columns in \(R\).
- Trace those pivot positions back to the corresponding columns of \(A\).
- Fade the nonpivot column.
- Conclude that
  \[
  \operatorname{span}\{\mathbf a_1,\mathbf a_2,\mathbf a_3\}
  =
  \operatorname{span}\{\mathbf a_1,\mathbf a_2\}.
  \]
- State that
  \[
  \operatorname{col}(A)=\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf a_1,\mathbf a_2\},
  \]
  so the original pivot columns form a basis for the column space.
