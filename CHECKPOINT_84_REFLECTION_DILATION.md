# Checkpoint 84 — Reflection Before or After Dilation

This revised CP84 demonstrates homogeneity through a specific geometric
experiment inspired by the attached lesson.

Two routes are compared:

\[
D_c(r_m(\mathbf v))
\]

and

\[
r_m(D_c(\mathbf v)).
\]

The animation shows that both routes end at the same vector:

\[
r_m(c\mathbf v)=c\,r_m(\mathbf v).
\]

This is the first condition of linearity.
