# Checkpoint 85 — Reflection Preserves Vector Addition

CP85 continues the geometric investigation from CP84.

Two routes are compared:

\[
r_m(\mathbf u+\mathbf v)
\]

and

\[
r_m(\mathbf u)+r_m(\mathbf v).
\]

The lesson first constructs \(\mathbf u+\mathbf v\), reflects the resultant,
then resets and reflects the two component vectors before adding them.

The coincident endpoints establish

\[
r_m(\mathbf u+\mathbf v)
=
r_m(\mathbf u)+r_m(\mathbf v),
\]

the additivity condition for linearity.
