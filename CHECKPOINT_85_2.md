# Checkpoint 85.2 — Retain the Reflected Sum

The animation now follows this sequence:

1. Draw \(\mathbf u\), \(\mathbf v\), and \(\mathbf u+\mathbf v\).
2. Reflect the resultant to obtain \(r_m(\mathbf u+\mathbf v)\).
3. Retain that reflected resultant.
4. Erase every other vector.
5. Redraw \(\mathbf u\), then reflect it.
6. Redraw \(\mathbf v\), then reflect it.
7. Translate \(r_m(\mathbf v)\) head-to-tail with \(r_m(\mathbf u)\).
8. Draw \(r_m(\mathbf u)+r_m(\mathbf v)\).
9. Show that this resultant coincides with the retained
   \(r_m(\mathbf u+\mathbf v)\).
