# Checkpoint 86.2 — Matrix–Vector Multiplication Bridge

The transformation card now displays a concrete matrix–vector product rather
than only the symbolic expression \(A\mathbf{x}\).

The final synthesis emphasizes:

\[
A(c\mathbf v)=c(A\mathbf v)
\]

and

\[
A(\mathbf u+\mathbf v)=A\mathbf u+A\mathbf v.
\]

This makes the pedagogical bridge explicit:

> Matrix multiplication preserves scaling and addition, so every matrix
> transformation is linear.
