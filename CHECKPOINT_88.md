# Checkpoint 88 — Inner Products and the Dot Product

## Purpose

Introduce the dot product through a visual question rather than a coordinate formula:

> Can two vectors produce a meaningful number?

The lesson begins with a fixed vector and a rotating comparison vector. A scalar
readout changes continuously, allowing students to notice positive, zero, and
negative values before seeing a formula.

## Pedagogical sequence

1. Recall that vector addition and scalar multiplication return vectors.
2. Ask whether two vectors can instead produce a scalar.
3. Rotate one vector and observe the changing value.
4. Connect positive, zero, and negative values with acute, right, and obtuse angles.
5. Reveal the coordinate formula.
6. Connect it with the geometric identity involving cosine.
7. Introduce orthogonality and the norm identity.
8. Identify the dot product as one example of the broader inner-product idea.
