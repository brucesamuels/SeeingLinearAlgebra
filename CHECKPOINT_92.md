# Checkpoint 92 — A Basis Determines the Transformation

The lesson constructs

\[
\mathbf x=2\mathbf e_1+\mathbf e_2
\]

and then shows

\[
T(\mathbf x)=2T(\mathbf e_1)+T(\mathbf e_2).
\]

A second example reinforces that the same basis images determine every output.
The closing bridge prepares CP93: the images of the basis vectors become the
columns of the transformation matrix.
