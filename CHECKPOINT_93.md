# Checkpoint 93 — From Basis Images to a Matrix

The lesson begins with \(T(\mathbf e_1)\) and \(T(\mathbf e_2)\), reads their
coordinates with dashed projections, and morphs copies of the two coordinate
columns into

\[
A=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}.
\]

It then reconnects the matrix columns to

\[
T(\mathbf x)=x_1T(\mathbf e_1)+x_2T(\mathbf e_2)
\]

and bridges to matrix-vector multiplication as a linear combination of columns.
