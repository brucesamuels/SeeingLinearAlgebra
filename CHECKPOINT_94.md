# Checkpoint 94 — Matrix–Vector Multiplication as a Column Combination

For

\[
A=\begin{bmatrix}\vert&\vert\\\mathbf a_1&\mathbf a_2\\\vert&\vert\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\mathbf x=\begin{bmatrix}x_1\\x_2\end{bmatrix},
\]

the product is

\[
A\mathbf x=x_1\mathbf a_1+x_2\mathbf a_2.
\]

The lesson reveals this symbolically, recreates it geometrically with
head-to-tail vectors, and then bridges to the row–column rule.
