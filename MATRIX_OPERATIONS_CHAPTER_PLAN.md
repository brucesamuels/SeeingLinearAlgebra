# Matrix Operations Chapter — Proposed Checkpoint Plan

The chapter should move from familiar entrywise operations to the more
structurally important idea that matrix multiplication represents composition.

## Proposed order

### CP96 — Matrix Addition and Subtraction
- Equal dimensions
- Corresponding entries
- Subtraction as addition of the negative
- Undefined dimension mismatch

### CP97 — Scalar Multiplication of Matrices
- Multiply every entry
- Geometric connection to scaling a transformation
- Distributive laws

### CP94 — Matrix–Vector Multiplication as a Column Combination
- Existing, completed lesson
- Place here as the third lesson rather than the chapter opener

### CP98 — The Row–Column Rule
- Recover each output entry from a row dotted with a column
- Connect directly to CP94 rather than presenting an unrelated trick

### CP99 — Matrix–Matrix Multiplication
- Dimension compatibility
- Compute every entry
- Product dimensions

### CP100 — Matrix Multiplication as Composition
- Apply one transformation, then another
- Explain order reversal in \(BA\mathbf{x}\)
- Visual before/after geometry

### CP101 — Matrix Multiplication Is Not Commutative
- Compare \(AB\) and \(BA\)
- Use transformations whose order visibly matters
- Identify cases where products may coincide without implying commutativity

### CP102 — Identity and Zero Matrices
- \(IA=AI=A\)
- \(A+0=A\)
- Distinguish additive and multiplicative identities
- Zero-product caution

### CP103 — Powers of a Matrix
- Repeated composition
- \(A^0=I\)
- Iterative/dynamical interpretation

### CP104 — Inverse Matrices
- Undoing a transformation
- \(A^{-1}A=AA^{-1}=I\)
- Invertibility and geometric collapse
- Preview solving \(A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}\)

### CP105 — Matrix Operations Chapter Assembly
- Opening card and chapter question
- Ordered lesson assembly: CP96, CP97, CP94, CP98–CP104
- Pacing, transitions, reflection, and chapter close
