## Motion

- Camera movement must have a mathematical purpose.
- Prefer morphing and compression to abrupt replacement.
- Rank changes should visibly collapse the reachable set.
- Use a reserved title band so labels and formulas are never obscured.

## Narration

- One mathematical claim per sentence.
- Leave pauses after conceptual questions.
- Introduce notation only after the geometry is visible.
